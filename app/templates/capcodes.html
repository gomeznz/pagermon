Capcodes

Label capcodes you know, such as your own test pagers, and give them a colour to pick them out on the Live and History screens. Tick Alert to send their pages to the webhook, or Suppress to hide a noisy capcode from the Live screen. Suppressed pages are still recorded and can be shown with Show suppressed. Use the Kiosks settings to forward, alert or suppress a capcode's pages on particular kiosk displays.

Keywords

Match words or phrases in the message text, on any capcode. A keyword can Alert, be Suppressed, or just get a colour; matches are highlighted in the message. Matching ignores case and, by default, only matches whole words (“fire” won't match “firewall”). Alerts always win: a page that alerts, by capcode or keyword, is never hidden on the Live screen. A keyword's colour takes priority over the capcode's. Both capcodes and keywords can also forward, alert or suppress pages on specific kiosks.
